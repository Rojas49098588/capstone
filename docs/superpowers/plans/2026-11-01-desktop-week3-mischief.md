# Desktop App, Week 3: Mischief Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Moderate pets get up to the four "easy" kinds of mischief from the spec: sticky notes anywhere on the desktop (gone after 15 minutes, sometimes carried in from the screen edge), chasing the mouse, opening their project in Studium without taking focus, and real sounds with a mute switch.

**Architecture:** Every rule (who, when, how often) is a pure function in `extension/content/logic.js`, with all numbers in one `MISCHIEF` object and a dev override (`petTimingOverride.mischiefMinutes`) for checking by hand. The overlay's copy of `pet.js` acts on them. The main process adds the cursor position (`overlay:cursor`) and the "open its project" timer (`src/main/mischief.js`), because it owns the Studium window. Sounds are short WAV placeholders, generated by a script, that the user can swap.

**Tech Stack:** Electron 45, Vitest 5, Playwright 1.64 `_electron`, Node 26. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-desktop-app-design.md` (Mischief, Anti-goals, Testing; Week 3 in "Order of work").

## Global Constraints

- Branch `feature/desktop-week3-mischief`, cut from `feature/nap-task-choice`, which holds Week 2 and the nap question.
- **Moderate only.** Nothing on Minimal. Mild behaves as today.
- **The quiet rules** (spec): no mischief while any pet naps (`PetLogic.isQuiet(petAway)`), while a check-in question is open (`petCheckIn`), while pets are hidden (`petHidden`), or from a pet that is away.
- **Never take keyboard focus.** "Open its project" shows Studium with `showInactive()` + `moveTop()`, and never restores a minimized window. Never type, close anything or touch files.
- Desktop only. In the browser extension (`PetSurface` `'page'`), notes behave as before and there is no chase and no sound. The habitat copy (`'habitat'`) gets no mischief.
- Numbers, in `MISCHIEF` (spec values):
  - `NOTE_STAY_MS` 15 min
  - `NOTE_CARRY_CHANCE` 0.3 (my call: "now and then")
  - chase every 10–20 min, about 10 s; gaps halved when Proud or Delighted; never Sick
  - open its project: Upset or Sick only, at most once every 2 h, not if Studium was showing in the last 30 min
  - sounds at most one every 30 s across all pets, volume 0.3
- Dev override: `petTimingOverride.mischiefMinutes` (a number from 0.05 to 15). When set, it replaces the chase gap, the open gap, the Studium-recent window, the note gap and the sound gap, for checking by hand. Same pattern as `napMinutes`.
- Tests first. At the end of every task: `npm test` and `npm run test:e2e` in `desktop/`, `npx vitest run --maxWorkers=4` and `npm run build` in `web/`. In Git Bash, prefix commands with `export PATH="/c/Program Files/nodejs:/c/Program Files/Git/cmd:$PATH"`, and unset `ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE`.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- **Pets growing up** (added to this week by the user) is **not in this plan**. It gets its own short design with the user (`superpowers:brainstorming`), then its own plan, after Task 6.

## Review Focus

1. **A nap starts in the middle of mischief** (a chase running, a note open, a sound queued): everything stops at once. The chasing pet goes idle, the note closes, and no sound plays. (Task 1 `mischiefAllowed`; Task 3 script test.)
2. **"Open its project" while the user is typing elsewhere:** focus never moves, and a minimized Studium stays minimized. (Task 4 `showStudiumQuietly` test with a fake window.)
3. **The cursor is off the overlay** (on the taskbar or a second monitor): the chase ends, and the pet doesn't run to the edge or out of the work area. (Task 3 `cursorInOverlay` → `null`.)
4. **Sounds when they shouldn't play:** on Mild, with Pet sounds off, or several pets at once. At most one sound every 30 s, and none at all when muted. (Task 5 `soundAllowed` + prefs tests.)
5. **Studium starting at login with an Upset pet, or the app restarting:** Studium doesn't pop up in the first 30 minutes, and a restart doesn't reset the 2-hour gap (`petOpenAt` is saved). (Task 4 tests.)

### Task 3: Chase the mouse

**Files:**

**Interfaces:**

- Produces:
  - `petBridge.cursor() -> Promise<{ x, y } | null>`: `ipc.invoke('overlay:cursor')`.

    - Each pet keeps `nextChaseAt`.

    - It pounces (`hop(1.4)` toward the cursor) when the cursor has stayed within 4 px for `POUNCE_STILL_MS` and is within `POUNCE_RANGE_PX`.
    - The chase ends after `CHASE_MS`, on a `null` cursor, when a nap starts, or on a drag. Then the pet goes idle and `nextChaseAt` is drawn again.
